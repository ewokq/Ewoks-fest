## v2.3 setlist changes

Four tracks were swapped for harder-hitting festival EDM with clearer build-ups and drops. We chose them by ccMixter recommendation counts, genre tags, measured loudness (EBU R128) and how clearly the drops were detected:

- *Mouse in my House* (Reiswerk, electro swing) → **Sailboat feat. snowflake** (Levihica, progressive house)
- *Mind (Robbero Mix)* (Robbero, nu-disco) → **Broken (Leap Of The Heart)** (7OOP3D, melodic dubstep)
- *Turning Into Normal* (SackJo22, techno) → **Hero** (VJ_Memes, dubstep / drum & bass, one of the most-recommended dance tracks on ccMixter)
- *Mine + Yours = Ours* (George_Ellinas, 2010 trance) → **Acceptance** (F_Fact, festival trance)

## Notes

- An earlier candidate ("Depart") was dropped in v2.2 because of a non-commercial sample, when the setlist was CC BY only. Since v2.3, CC BY-NC material is allowed because the game is free and non-commercial. Anyone reusing these tracks commercially must check each licence first.
- The game's other tracks are generated live by its own WebAudio synth engine and contain no third-party audio.
- **My Song** mode plays YouTube videos that the player chooses. That content is not distributed with this game.

Thank you to every artist above and to the ccMixter community. To reuse these tracks, follow each track's licence terms and credit the artists as shown.
