## Notes

- One candidate track ("Depart") was **removed** because one of its samples ("April") is licensed CC BY-NC 3.0, which is non-commercial and doesn't fit this project's licensing.
- The game's other tracks (Progressive, Big Room, Mainstage, Tech House, Melodic Techno, Peak Techno, Trance, DnB, Dubstep, Bass House, Future Rave and Atmospheric) are generated live by the game's own WebAudio synth engine and contain no third-party audio.
- The **My Song** mode plays YouTube videos that the player chooses; that content is not distributed with this game.

Thank you to every artist above and to the ccMixter community. To use these tracks yourself, follow the CC BY 3.0 attribution terms and credit each artist as shown.
