# FESTIVAL CONTROL v2.2

You are the lighting and show operator of an EDM festival mainstage. Run the moving lights, lasers, LED walls, haze and simulated pyro live to a procedural EDM set, keep the crowd hyped, and get scored like a real show operator.

**Play:** https://ewokq.github.io/Ewoks-fest/ (works on iPhone Safari in portrait or landscape, and on desktop)
**Old game (v1.4 Drop Control 3D):** https://ewokq.github.io/Ewoks-fest/classic/

- **New in v2.2:** the Career setlist is now 10 real Creative Commons (CC BY 3.0) EDM tracks from ccMixter, analysed offline so the scoring, hints, DROP countdown, lights and crowd follow each song (credits in CREDITS.md and the in-game Music credits screen). The synth engine was rebuilt with supersaw, sidechain pumping, layered kicks, risers and impacts, vocal chops, growls and a mastering chain. New **Multiplayer**: challenge links (same track, stage, seed and difficulty, checksummed), local top-10 leaderboards, and pass-the-phone party mode for 2–8 players with a podium.
- **v2.1:** a big PLAY button on a new main menu; a guided first show; a Simple console with 12 big labelled pads (tap **PRO** for the full console); on-screen hints, a drop countdown, glowing "tap me" pads, PERFECT/GREAT timing popups with combos, haptics and sounds; pyro arms itself; a shorter 8-step tutorial with a pointer; and a much livelier crowd (real human figures with their own beat phase, fist pumps, build-up claps, unison jumps on the drop, circle pits, shoulder riders, flags and totems, cheer waves, phone lights and sway in breakdowns, crowd audio, and confetti that lands on the crowd). Settings → Gameplay has Simple/Pro, Hints and Auto-arm pyro.
- Modes: Career (7 venues and a shop), Show, Free Play sandbox, Challenges, Tutorial, My Song (YouTube, audio only, Auto BPM)
- Desktop keys: Space play/pause · Q console · E FX · R LED · Tab dashboard · J flash · L strobe · K blackout · Enter GO · P arm pyro · T/Y/N/M/G pyro · 1–8 groups · V camera · Esc menu
- See ROADMAP.md for spec coverage and limitations.

Venues, DJs and gear are fictional. The real Career tracks belong to their artists and are used under CC BY 3.0 (see CREDITS.md).
